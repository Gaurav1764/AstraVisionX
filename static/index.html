📖 Understanding YOLO Confidence Scores Honestly

• Model match score, not truth probability: A YOLO detection confidence reflects how strongly visual features in a proposed box match the trained class representation, not the formal mathematical probability of ground-truth correctness.

• Typical 65% – 90% range: In crowded, outdoor, or complex natural scenes, valid detections regularly score between 65% and 90%. Scores in this range are normal and expected.

• Why scores drop: Small or distant objects, partial occlusion/overlap by nearby items, camera blur, or atypical poses naturally reduce boundary activation scores.

• Tiers are guides: The High (≥80%), Good (65-79%), Moderate (50-64%), and Uncertain (<50%) labels provide consistent operational guidance, but are decision heuristics rather than diagnostic guarantees.
